Common Questions

Before you reach out

The questions we hear most from dentists thinking about ownership for the first time.

Do I need business experience to do this?

No. Most dentists we work with have never run a business before — that's the point of working with us. Our founders bring their own DSO-level experience in site selection, construction, and operations, so you don't have to learn it all yourself before you start.

How much does this cost?

Every project is different, so we walk through real numbers on your free consultation rather than quoting a one-size-fits-all price here. Fees are staged around project milestones — a deposit to start, a payment at building permit issuance, and a final payment after opening — so you're never paying for work that hasn't happened yet.

How long does it take from start to opening day?

Most projects run 6 to 10 months from your first site visit to opening day, depending on how quickly permits and construction move in your specific location.

Do you only work in Toronto or the GTA?

No — we work across Ontario. We have real market experience in practically every Ontario city and town, including Ottawa (which carries a small additional fee due to travel and local permitting).

What if I don't know where to open yet?

That's usually where we start. Site acquisition and financial planning — location strategy, feasibility, and lease negotiation — is the first phase of every project.

Who actually does the work — you, or a list of contractors I have to manage?

We do. PracticeWise is a single point of responsibility for your entire project — design, permits, construction management, equipment, and marketing all run through one team, so there's no finger-pointing between vendors.

What happens after my clinic opens — am I on my own?

No. We stay hands-on for one month after opening — marketing optimization, staffing support, and operational guidance — so your first weeks as an owner aren't spent troubleshooting alone.

I'm still not sure I'm ready. Can I still talk to you?

Yes — that's exactly what the free consultation is for. No pressure, no obligation. Many dentists we talk to aren't ready to sign anything; they just want an honest, experienced read on whether their idea is realistic.
